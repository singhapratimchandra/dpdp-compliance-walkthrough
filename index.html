What the 13 November 2026 milestone expects of you

Today is the second of three commencement dates. It opens Consent Manager registration and nothing else. If you plan to become a Consent Manager, the full checklist below applies now. If you are any other business, nothing new is legally due today, and your real deadline is 13 May 2027.

In force13 Nov 2025

Rules 1, 2, 17 to 21. Data Protection Board established in law.

Today13 Nov 2026

Rule 4 and the First Schedule. Act sections 6(9) and 27(1)(d).

6 months away13 May 2027

Rules 3, 5 to 16, 22, 23. Notice, consent, security, breach, rights, children, SDFs, penalties.

What starts today, and what does not

Starts 13 November 2026

  • Consent Managers can apply to the Board for registration Rule 4(1)
  • Registration conditions and ongoing obligations apply First Schedule A, B
  • Every Consent Manager must be registered with the Board s.6(9)
  • The Board can inquire into a Consent Manager's breach of its obligations or registration conditions s.27(1)(d)

Waits until 13 May 2027

  • Notice and consent duties Rule 3, ss.5-6
  • Security safeguards and breach reporting Rules 6-7
  • Erasure, retention, contact details Rules 8-9
  • Children's data, SDF duties, Data Principal rights Rules 10-14
  • Monetary penalties ss.33-34

Check before you rely on this

  • Board readiness. The latest reports I could find (August to October 2026) said no Chairperson or Members had been appointed and no registration portal was described. The Board must also publish the application particulars and the platform standards used for certification. Confirm the current position on the Board and MeitY websites.
  • Exact date. Rule 4 starts one year after Gazette publication. Most sources give publication as 13 November 2025; a few say 14 November. Confirm against the Gazette copy.
  • Proposed acceleration. In early 2026 MeitY floated moving the 18-month deadline up to November 2026, by some accounts for Significant Data Fiduciaries only. As of October 2026 no amendment had been gazetted. If one has been since, Track B is due today for those it covers.
  • Wording. Checklist items paraphrase the First Schedule. Read the Gazette text for anything you file.
