# DPDP compliance walkthrough

A visual walkthrough of what India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025 expect at the 13 November 2026 commencement date.

Open `index.html` in a browser. It covers:

- the three commencement dates and what starts on each
- a staged, tickable checklist for Consent Manager applicants (Rule 4 and the First Schedule)
- the preparation other Data Fiduciaries should have under way for 13 May 2027

This is a planning aid, not legal advice. Checklist items paraphrase the Rules; read the Gazette text before relying on them.
